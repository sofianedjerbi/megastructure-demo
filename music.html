The game's music: final mix

The game's music after the final mix. Every piece is at the same loudness (-20 LUFS, a background-music level, under the main sound effects), peaks under the ceiling, bass and kick centred, a gentle glue compressor on the day pieces and the boss. Far shore night is in two new versions (C, D) on other instruments. The sound effects are levelled on the same scale. These are in the game: day and night pieces play now and then with silence between, Old Mossback loops during the fight (Far shore night C is the one wired in).

Earlier: bright plucks, one piece on three palettes

The direction you found better (bright plucks), designed further: one piece of about a minute with a form (intro, A, B, A), fills, a second sound answering the tune, a bass audible on small speakers, and the band ducking under the kick. The same piece on three sets of Surge XT sounds, so the sounds and the writing can be judged apart.

Earlier: five directions, about 20 s each

New approach: the music is still written in Python, but played on Surge XT, a real synthesizer, with sounds made by sound designers. Five short loops in five different directions (rhythm, sounds and tone all differ). Which direction, if any, is worth continuing?
